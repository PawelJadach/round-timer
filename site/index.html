Sparring · Timer · Stopwatch

Rounds, rest, and a ring bell.

Sparring, a countdown, and a stopwatch. A dark face, a glow for each phase, and no account.

Open the clock

In the phone browser. The clock’s own screen is in Polish. On iPhone, English and Polish follow the phone.

The card

Three modes.

Same clock in the browser and on iPhone. Pick a mode, then start. Pause, resume, and reset are on the face. When a countdown or sparring session ends, Again runs it once more.

01

Sparring

Prepare, work, rest, and rounds. The face shows the round, and the setup shows the full session length. Rest is skipped after the last round.

Your own times: prepare up to 2:00, work from 5 seconds to 60:00, rest up to 30:00, rounds from 1 to 30.

02

Timer

Counts down. Presets run from 30 seconds to 10 minutes, or set minutes and seconds yourself.

It starts at 5 seconds or more, up to 24 hours.

03

Stopwatch

Time counts up, to the hundredth of a second. Lap stores the split and the total.

Controls: Start, Pause, Resume, Reset, Again. On the stopwatch: Lap.

Presets

Four sparring cards, five countdowns.

  • Tabata

    20/10 × 8

    5 seconds to prepare, 20 seconds of work, 10 seconds of rest, 8 rounds.

  • Interval

    30/15 × 10

    5 seconds to prepare, 30 seconds of work, 15 seconds of rest, 10 rounds.

  • Sparring

    3:00 / 1:00 × 5

    10 seconds to prepare, 3:00 of work, 1:00 of rest, 5 rounds.

  • Round

    5:00 / 1:00 × 3

    10 seconds to prepare, 5:00 of work, 1:00 of rest, 3 rounds.

Countdown presets: 0:30, 1:00, 3:00, 5:00, and 10:00. Sound, volume, and these times stay on the device.

The face

The glow follows the phase.

The labels on the clock are short and uppercase. The color behind them changes with the phase.

  • Prepare

    Gold. “Starting soon.”

  • Work

    Red. The round itself.

  • Rest

    Blue. Between rounds.

  • End

    Green. “Session complete,” or “Time’s up” on a countdown.

  • Pause

    The face reads Pause until you resume or reset.

  • Stopwatch

    A light face. “Counting up,” or the lap number.

The bell

A cue you can hear with the gloves on.

In the browser, work, rest, and the end use a ring bell. Prepare, the start of a countdown, and the last three seconds are short tones. Those ticks play when a segment lasts at least five seconds, and not during prepare. A lap plays a tick if sound is on. Sound and volume can be turned down or off, and that choice is remembered.

On iPhone, each cue is a short sound that ducks other music for a moment, then the mix comes back. YouTube keeps playing. With the screen off, the round cue arrives as a notification if you allow it. A short haptic plays with the cue. The screen stays on while a session is running. The phase and time can sit on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island when Live Activities are on.

In the browser, while a session is running, the page asks the browser to keep the screen awake.

Where it runs

Browser, and iPhone.

Browser

  • Opens in the phone browser. No account.
  • You can add it to the home screen.
  • After it has loaded once, it keeps a copy of its files for a later visit.
  • The clock’s own interface is in Polish.
  • Settings stay on that device.

iPhone

  • iOS 17 or later. No account.
  • English and Polish follow the system language.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, when Live Activities are on.
  • Notifications are the backup cue if the system suspends the app.
  • Share a link to the clock from Settings. About links to privacy, terms, and support.

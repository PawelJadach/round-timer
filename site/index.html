Open the clock

English and Polish, in the browser and on the phone.

Modes

Three modes.

Rounds

Prepare, work, rest, and how many times. Nothing follows the last round.

Timer

Counts down. Five seconds at the shortest, up to 24 hours.

Stopwatch

Counts up, to the hundredth. Lap keeps the split and the total.

Presets

Two presets.

Interval 30/15 × 10 5s prepare
Sparring 3:00 / 1:00 × 5 10s prepare

Or set it yourself. Prepare up to 2:00, work from 5 seconds to 60:00, rest up to 30:00, rounds from 1 to 30. In the browser and on Android, the timer also has 0:30, 1:00, 3:00, 5:00, and 10:00.

The face

Color is the phase.

  • Prepare

    Gold. Starting soon.

  • Work

    Red. The round.

  • Rest

    Blue. Between rounds.

  • End

    Session complete, or time’s up.

Sound

A bell, then three ticks.

Work, rest, and the end use the ring bell. The last three seconds tick when that stretch lasts at least five seconds. Prepare does not tick. Sound and volume stay on the device.

On iPhone the cue ducks other music, then the mix comes back. Screen off, the cue arrives as a notification. The time can sit on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Where

Browser, iPhone, Android.

Browser

Opens on the phone. English or Polish from the switch on the clock. Add it to the home screen. Settings stay on that device.

iPhone

iOS 17 or later. Same two languages. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island when Live Activities are on.

Android

The same three modes. A running session keeps the bell playing if you leave the app.

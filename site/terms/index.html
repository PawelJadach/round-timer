Terms of use

Updated 13 November 2026.

These terms cover this website, the browser clock at mma-clock.vercel.app, and the Round Timer app for iPhone.

Using the clock

The clock is free to use. You do not need an account. Settings stay on your device.

What you get

The browser clock runs in a web browser. The iPhone app needs iOS 17 or later. Both are offered as they are. A phone can lock or suspend an app. On iPhone, if you allow notifications, those alerts are the backup cue when that happens. There is no promise that a cue will sound in every case.

A timer, not a coach

Round Timer counts time. It does not coach, officiate, or give medical advice. You decide how you train.

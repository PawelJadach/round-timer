# Round Timer — privacy notes (English)

For the Play Console listing. This file is not a hosted privacy-policy URL. Do not mark the app as submitted.

## What the app does with data

Round Timer does not create an account and does not send timers, settings, or identifiers to a server. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

These things stay on the phone:

- Prepare, work, rest, rounds, and countdown length
- Sound on or off, and volume
- The language you pick (English or Polish), if you use the switch

Android may show a notification while a session is running, and the app holds a short wake lock so the round clock and the bell still fire if you leave the app or the screen turns off. That notification is local. It is not sent anywhere.

The only network use is if you tap the bell credit. That opens freesound.org or creativecommons.org in the browser.

## Play Console Data safety

Answer “no” for data collected and data shared. The app does not collect personal information, location, audio recordings, or crash logs.

Permissions:

- Foreground service (media playback), so cues can play after you leave the app
- Notifications, for the ongoing session notice
- Wake lock, so the clock does not sleep through a round

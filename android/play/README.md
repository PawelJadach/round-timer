Listing files for a manual Google Play upload. Nothing in this folder is submitted to Play Console.

- `en-US/` and `pl-PL/` are the store title, short description, and full description.
- `privacy-en.md` and `privacy-pl.md` are the privacy notes and the Data safety answers. The app does not collect or share data. Hosted privacy policy URLs: https://round-timer-marketing.vercel.app/privacy/ (English) and https://round-timer-marketing.vercel.app/pl/privacy/ (Polish).
- `graphics/icon-512.png` is the 512×512 icon. `graphics/feature-1024x500.png` is the feature graphic.
- `graphics/phone/` are phone screenshots in English and Polish—the same native device captures used in the App Store kit (`en-rounds`, `en-timer`, `en-stopwatch`, `pl-rounds`, `pl-timer`, `pl-stopwatch`). They are not drawings or emulator art.

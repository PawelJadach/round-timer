Listing files for a manual Google Play upload. Nothing in this folder is submitted to Play Console.

- `en-US/` and `pl-PL/` are the store title, short description, and full description.
- `privacy-en.md` and `privacy-pl.md` are the privacy notes and the Data safety answers. The app does not collect or share data. There is no hosted privacy-policy URL here.
- `graphics/icon-512.png` is the 512×512 icon. `graphics/feature-1024x500.png` is the feature graphic.
- `graphics/phone/` are listing images in the app’s layout and both languages. They were drawn to match the UI. A debug build was installed on an emulator: the accessibility tree showed sparring setup (presets, 5-second values, volume 80) and, after Start, a live round with Reset and Pause and the mode tabs hidden. The emulator framebuffer stayed blank, so these are not device captures. Swap in phone photos before upload if you want camera shots.
